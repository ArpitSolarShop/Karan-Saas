# 🏢 B2B Enterprise Features: Companies, Approvals & Workbooks

Welcome to the Enterprise Suite of Project Alpha CRM! This guide covers the advanced tools designed for B2B (Business-to-Business) sales teams.

---

## 1. Managing Companies (B2B Accounts)

Instead of just tracking individual people (Leads), you can now track entire organizations and link multiple leads to them.

### Adding a New Company
1. Click **Companies** (building icon) in the left sidebar.
2. Click **+ Add Company** in the top right.
3. Fill in the **Company Name**, **Industry**, **Website**, and **Size**. 
4. Click **Save**. The company is now in your registry.

### Linking Leads to Companies
When you add a new lead (person), you want to associate them with their employer:
1. Go to **Leads** and click **+ Add Lead**.
2. In the registration form, look for the **Company / Account** searchable dropdown.
3. Type the company name and select it from the list.
4. When you save, the lead is permanently linked. Now, viewing the Company profile will show all employees you are selling to.

## 2. The Approvals Inbox

Enterprise deals often require manager sign-off for large discounts, SLAs, or leave requests. The **Approval Inbox** handles all of this automatically.

### How to Request Approval
1. When you change a deal stage to "Contract Sent" with a discount over your allowed limit, the system will *automatically* generate an approval request.
2. The deal will be locked in a "Pending Approval" state.

### How Managers Approve Requests
1. If you are a Manager, click **Approvals** (shield icon) in the left sidebar.
2. You will see a grid of all pending requests (e.g., "Enterprise Deal Discount (20%)").
3. Click the green **✓ Approve** or red **✕ Reject** button.
4. The agent is instantly notified, and the workflow resumes!

---

## 3. Workbooks (Live Spreadsheets)

Need to crunch numbers, track complex data, or securely import massive lists without leaving your CRM? Workbooks are built-in, real-time spreadsheets.

### Using Workbooks
1. Click **Workbooks** in the sidebar.
2. Open a workbook (e.g., "Q1 Campaign Targets").
3. You will see a live spreadsheet. You can click on any cell and edit it instantly, just like Excel or Google Sheets.
4. **Real-time Sync**: If another agent is looking at the same workbook, they will see your typed changes instantly.
5. **Data Protection**: If you try to type a phone number that is on the company "Do Not Call" (DNC) list, the cell will reject your change and show an error notification.

---

## Summary of Enterprise Habits
* **Always Link:** Ensure every high-value Lead is attached to a Company. It helps segment your reporting.
* **Check Approvals Daily:** If you are a manager, clear your Approval Inbox daily so your agents can close their deals.
* **Stay in the CRM:** Instead of using external spreadsheets, use Workbooks so your data stays secure inside Project Alpha.
